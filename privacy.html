Privacy Policy

Draft: This policy is being prepared for release.

You can play as a guest without signing in. Guest gameplay progress and notification preference are saved locally on this device. The project contains no developer account service, online save, analytics, advertising, or network API.

If you opt in to low-stock reminders, the game asks the operating system for notification permission and schedules reminders locally from your machine's stock. Notification content and gameplay data are not sent to MACHI servers. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in the device's notification settings.

Apple Game Center and Google Play Games are not integrated in this build, so MACHI does not currently receive their player identifiers or profile data. If either service is added, update this policy to explain the data the provider processes, what MACHI receives, why it is used, and how long it is retained.
