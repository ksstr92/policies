Little Motor Town Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Little Motor Town has no game account, login, ads, analytics or tracking. Shiken Lab does not collect personal information through gameplay. The game does not ask for children’s names or contact details, record their voices, or access their location, camera or contacts.

What stays on your device

The game stores preferences such as sound and language, play-time settings, and a local verifier for any parent PIN you set. We use these to remember your choices and enforce parent controls. They are not sent to Shiken Lab. To remove this data, delete the app or clear the game’s site data in your browser. Device backups may retain a copy according to your settings.

App Store purchases

Apple handles billing and payment for App Store purchases, including any in-app purchases. Shiken Lab does not receive your card details or Apple Account credentials. The app uses Apple’s purchase verification on your device to unlock and restore content. In-app purchase prompts are protected by a parent gate. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice for how Apple handles purchase information.

Narration and websites

The game may use your device’s text-to-speech service to read game text. Depending on the voice you use, the service may process that text online. This does not use the microphone. Turn sound off to disable narration.

If you play the browser version or visit this policy online, the website host may process technical details such as your IP address to deliver and secure the page. We do not use this information to track gameplay or for advertising.

Questions or deletion requests

Parents and caregivers can email hello@shikenlab.com. We use your email address and message to respond and retain them only as long as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. Please avoid sending a child’s personal information. Contact us if you believe a child sent us personal information, or to request access, correction or deletion of support correspondence.
